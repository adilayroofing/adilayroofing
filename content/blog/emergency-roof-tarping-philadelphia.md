## When a Roof Needs Emergency Tarping

Tarping is the right response when the roof envelope is open and weather is coming. The common Philadelphia scenarios:

- **Wind-lifted or missing shingles.** Nor'easter and thunderstorm gusts over 60 mph lift shingle tabs, crease them, or strip whole sections — especially on older three-tab shingles. If underlayment or bare deck is exposed, the next rain comes inside.
- **Tree and limb strikes.** A limb through the deck is the most urgent case: the hole is large, and the structure around it may be compromised.
- **Hail-cracked or punctured surfaces.** Less common in Philadelphia than wind damage, but hail can crack aged shingles and puncture flat-roof membranes.
- **Flat-roof membrane failures.** On Philadelphia rowhomes, wind can get under a poorly adhered EPDM edge and peel the membrane back like a lid. Tarping a flat roof is a different technique than tarping a slope, and it's one reason to use a roofer who knows rowhome roofs.
- **Fire or structural damage.** After a fire, tarping protects the interior from weather while the insurance and rebuild process runs.

The test is simple: **can water reach the inside of the building?** If yes, tarp now, repair after. If the damage is cosmetic — a few granules, a bent gutter — you can skip the tarp and go straight to a scheduled [roof repair](/services/roof-repair).

## What Professional Tarping Actually Involves

There's a right way to tarp a roof, and it's the difference between a cover that survives six weeks of weather and one that fails in the next storm.

1. **Assessment and safety check.** The crew confirms the deck around the damage can be walked on, and identifies where the water is actually entering — which is not always where the visible damage is.
2. **Debris clearing.** Limbs, loose shingles, and torn membrane get cleared so the tarp lies flat. A tarp over debris chafes through in days.
3. **Heavy-duty tarp, correctly oriented.** We use 6-mil reinforced poly, sized so the tarp extends well past the damage in every direction — and critically, over the ridge or up-slope far enough that water flowing down the roof travels **onto** the tarp, never under its top edge.
4. **Mechanical securing.** The tarp edges get wrapped around 1x4 furring strips and screwed down, or ballasted with sandbags on flat roofs where fasteners would create new holes. Bricks tossed on a loose tarp — the classic DIY move — become projectiles in the next wind event.
5. **Documentation.** Photos of the damage before and after tarping. You'll want these for the insurance claim, and so will we if we're helping you with it.

The whole job typically takes one to two hours. When you call [our emergency line](/services/emergency-roof-repair), we aim for same-day installation anywhere in Philadelphia County and the close suburbs.

## How the Insurance Side Works

Homeowner's policies in Pennsylvania generally cover sudden storm damage — wind, hail, falling trees — and they include a duty to **mitigate**: you're required to take reasonable steps to stop the damage from spreading. Emergency tarping is the standard, expected form of mitigation, which is why carriers routinely reimburse it.

Practical steps that make reimbursement smooth:

- **Photograph everything** before the tarp goes on: the roof damage, any interior water, the debris.
- **Keep the tarping invoice.** Submit it with the claim as a mitigation expense.
- **Don't wait for the adjuster to tarp.** Some homeowners leave the roof open for days "so the adjuster can see it." Photos preserve the evidence; an open roof just grows the interior damage — which the carrier may then attribute to your inaction.
- **Get the permanent repair documented independently.** A detailed contractor's scope-of-work and photo set gives the adjuster something concrete to work against. We provide this documentation on every storm job, and we'll [work directly with your carrier](/services/storm-damage-roof-repair) if needed.

One more honest note: if your roof was already at the end of its life, a storm claim won't buy you a new roof — carriers pay for storm damage, not wear. An inspection tells you which situation you're in before you file.

## Tarp Is a Bridge, Not a Destination

A tarp's job is to hold for 30–60 days while the claim processes and the permanent fix gets scheduled. Plan the permanent repair the same week the tarp goes on:

- **Localized damage** on an otherwise healthy roof → targeted [roof repair](/services/roof-repair), often same-week.
- **Widespread damage or an aging roof** → replacement makes more financial sense than chasing repairs. See our [2026 Philadelphia roof replacement cost guide](/blog/roof-replacement-cost-philadelphia-2026) for real numbers.
