## Exciting News for Philadelphia Homeowners

We're proud to announce that Adilay Roofing is now an authorized contractor through **Service Finance Company, LLC** — a nationally licensed sales finance company and FHA Title I Lender. This means Philadelphia homeowners can now finance their roofing projects with flexible, affordable payment options.

## What This Means for You

A new roof is one of the most important investments you can make in your home, but we understand it's also a significant expense. With roof financing through Service Finance Company, you don't have to choose between protecting your home and protecting your savings.

Here's what our financing partnership offers:

- **Loans from $1,000 to $100,000** — covering everything from minor repairs to full roof replacements
- **50+ loan products** — including same-as-cash promotional periods with 0% interest
- **No payments until your job is complete** — you don't pay a cent until you're satisfied with the work
- **Fast credit decisions** — often the same day you apply
- **No prepayment penalties** — pay off your loan early with no extra fees
- **Unsecured loans** — your home is not used as collateral

## Who Can Apply?

Service Finance Company works with a **range of credit profiles**, so even if your credit isn't perfect, you may still qualify. The application process is quick — just a few minutes online or over the phone — and checking your eligibility typically does not affect your credit score.
