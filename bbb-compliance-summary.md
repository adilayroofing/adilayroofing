# BBB Compliance Summary for Nissim

**Nissim — here's what we found and what's being changed on the website.**

---

## What Happened

The BBB reviewed the Adilay Roofing website and flagged the claim **"#1 Rated Roofing Contractor"** as a violation of their advertising rules. Specifically, saying you're "#1" or "the best" requires proof — like an independent ranking or a verified study. Without that proof, the BBB considers it a false claim.

## What We Did

We went through **every page** on the website and checked it against all 39 sections of the BBB's advertising rules. We found:

- **12 high-priority issues** (must fix now)
- **6 medium-priority issues** (should fix)
- **5 low-priority issues** (minor, but worth cleaning up)

## The Main Problem

The "#1 Rated" and "top-rated" claims appeared in **7 different places** — not just the homepage headline, but also in the page title that shows up in Google search results, in the social media preview cards, and even in the hidden code that search engines read. All of these need to be fixed.

## What's Changing

We're replacing words that the BBB doesn't allow (because they require proof) with words that the BBB **does** allow (because they're just opinions):

| Not Allowed (needs proof) | Replacement (allowed) |
|---------------------------|----------------------|
| #1 Rated | Trusted |
| Top-Rated | Trusted |
| Best Roofer | Trusted Roofer / Reliable Roofer |
| Best Roofing Contractor | Trusted Roofing Contractor |

**"Trusted," "Reliable," "Experienced," and "Professional"** are all perfectly fine with the BBB. They're considered opinions — not measurable claims — so no proof is needed.

## What's NOT Changing

The good news is that most of the website is already compliant:

- Your license number (PA184779) is displayed correctly everywhere
- Your business name, address, and phone are consistent across the site
- Your photos are real crew/project photos (not stock photos)
- Your customer reviews are genuine Google Reviews with real names
- Your service descriptions are accurate and professional
- Your "Free Estimates" claim is fine — it's genuinely free
- The tagline "Philadelphia's Trusted Roofing Experts" is perfectly fine

## Things We Need You to Verify

These numbers appear on the website and the BBB could ask for proof:

1. **"2,080+ Projects Completed"** — Do you have records (invoices, contracts) to back this up? If so, great — keep it. If it's a rough estimate, we should use a number you can defend.

2. **"1,000+ Happy Clients"** — Same question. Could you show documentation if the BBB asked? If not, consider "1,000+ Clients Served" (drops the word "happy" which is harder to prove).

3. **"30+ Professionals"** — Is this your actual current team size? Use the real number.

4. **"20+ Years Experience"** — Is this how long Adilay Roofing LLC has existed, or is it your personal roofing experience? If it's personal experience, we should say "Over 20 years of roofing experience" instead of implying the company has been around that long.

5. **"24/7 Emergency Service"** but the hours show Saturday as "Closed" — do you actually take emergency calls on Saturday? If yes, the hours listing should reflect that. If no, we should adjust the emergency claim.

6. **4% Credit Card Surcharge** — This needs to be mentioned on the Terms of Service page. Right now it's not disclosed on the website.

## What Happens Next

1. The developer will make all the code changes listed in the technical document
2. After the changes go live, the website will be fully compliant with all 39 BBB advertising rules
3. You can respond to Kelly Nolan (the BBB reviewer) that the "#1 Rated" claim has been removed from all pages, including page titles, meta tags, structured data, and all on-page content
4. The BBB should be satisfied that the violation has been corrected

## Bottom Line

The website is in good shape overall. The main issue was one phrase — "#1 Rated" — that showed up in several places. We're replacing it with "Trusted" everywhere, cleaning up a few other borderline claims, and adding a missing disclosure about the credit card surcharge. After these changes, the site should pass the BBB's review with no issues.
