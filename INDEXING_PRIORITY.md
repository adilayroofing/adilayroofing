# Indexing Priority — Service-Area Pages

**Goal:** unstick the 69 service-area pages from "Pending" validation in Google Search Console by manually requesting indexing in tiered batches.

**How to use this list:**
> Open Search Console → URL Inspection → paste URL → "Test Live URL" → "Request Indexing".
> Cap at 10/day to avoid GSC rate limits. After requesting, check the box to track progress.
> Days are sequential — start at Day 1 and work down.

**Tiering rationale:**
- **Tier 1:** Philadelphia + 9 highest-volume neighborhoods (closest to business location, biggest search demand).
- **Tier 2:** County hub pages (PA + NJ).
- **Tier 3:** Major suburbs with strong commercial/residential intent.
- **Tier 4:** Mid-tier suburbs.
- **Tier 5:** Smaller boroughs and remaining cities.
